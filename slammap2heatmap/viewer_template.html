Localizability

Read this first

Every coloured cell answers one question: if a camera stood here, would it know where it is? Bright means yes.

That splits in two, and the layers below let you see which half fails. Relocalization is finding yourself from nothing — the hard part, and what fails when a device will not start up in a room. Tracking is keeping hold of it once found.

The map was built from a handful of fixed scan positions (the rings). Quality falls off with distance from them, and it depends on which way you face — so pick how headings are combined below, and click any cell to see all of them.

Orange diamonds, where shown, are the best places to put the next scan position — ranked by how much of the weak area each would rescue.
